## Abstract

Myron Xie and Jordan Nanos discuss NVIDIA's Rubin Ultra HBM configuration: a preview once pointed at 1TB per package while the part that ships is described as carrying 192GB. Show notes frame the cut as supply-driven rather than performance-driven, and argue that shipping less memory per chip can still be the correct economic choice — "for the first time in recent memory," a next flagship with less capacity than what it replaces.
