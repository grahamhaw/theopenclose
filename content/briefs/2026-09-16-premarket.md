## Positioning

**AI capex durability — operators and labs still framing pacing as more compute, not a guide-down.** Baker’s overnight [extension](/discussions/2026-09-14-baker-pacing-compute-margins) cites OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar (“getting more compute to keep that flywheel going”) and Sachin Katti (“even more compute” for safety/alignment) against Monday’s infrastructure selloff; pushback holds that pacing is about *when* spend lands, not the level. Nadella on [All-In](/longform/2026-09-15-allin-satya-nadella-ai-pacing) treats common-sense control + diffusion as compatible with build/lease/rent kit matching (~60% of cost asr). SemiAnalysis [Ep.031](/longform/2026-09-16-semianalysis-ep031-pace-frontier) inverts “pace the frontier” into higher safety/eval intensity (OpenAI CoT monitoring ~20% of rollup compute asr) while physical bring-online stays below demand for 2–3 years. SemiAnalysis’s [moratoriums map](/news/2026-09-16-semianalysis-dc-moratoriums-mapped) — 1,525MW slip vs 20GW inside restricted boundaries — separates permit friction from cancelled hyperscaler spend. No top-four sequential capex guide-down in window; oil + ~5% 10y remain competing discount-rate pressure into the Fed, not a theme falsifier alone.

**HBM supply binds — onshoring talks, not near-term wafers.** [CNBC/Reuters](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/intel-sk-hynix-us-memory-chips.html) exploratory Hynix–Intel U.S. memory path (Ohio lease and/or cloud-inclusive JV) sits beside Hynix’s already-announced $4B Indiana HBM plant; company relay says no plans confirmed. Room debate ([discussion](/discussions/2026-09-16-hynix-intel-hyperscaler-memory-jv)) correctly weights hyperscaler JV structure over the bilateral headline — still talks ≠ process transfer, tools, or yield this quarter. Theme’s third-supplier / architecture-cut falsifiers unmet.

**Inference margin inversion — contested TCO snapshots, no lab GM print.** SemiAnalysis says MI355X is [closing](/discussions/2026-09-16-amd-mi355x-vs-gb300-inference-tco) agentic-inference perf/TCO vs GB300 via SGLang/MoRI/UMBP; replies’ InferenceX $/M-token cites still favor GB300 on other models/SLOs and treat the delta as stack maturity. No consecutive price-cut gross-margin disclosure from a frontier lab overnight.
