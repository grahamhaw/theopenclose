**HBM supply binds — contested on new axes, not cleared.** Samsung [dual-sourcing](https://x.com/jukan05/status/2099770851516428455) custom HBM base dies via TSMC ([discussion](/discussions/2026-09-15-samsung-hbm-base-die-tsmc-dual-source)) is customer-access / NVHBM plumbing — not a third stack supplier ending the constraint; replies flag TSMC advanced-logic wafer allocation as scarcity transfer.
