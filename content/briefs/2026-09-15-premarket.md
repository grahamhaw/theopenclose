## The lead

Overnight the problem set rotated from Monday's AI-essay de-rating toward the Fed's rate path under three-digit oil. The 10-year Treasury yield rose as much as four basis points to 5.02% on Tuesday — highest since 2007 on the Bloomberg print — after Monday's touch near 5.01–5.014% and a close near 4.96% (Bloomberg / prior desk postmarket). Brent and WTI stayed elevated on Saudi East-West pipeline outage risk after fresh Houthi strikes: Reuters near the US open had Brent ~$105.74 / WTI ~$101.66 after session highs near ~$108.43 / ~$104.21; Markets Insider ~9:25am ET showed Brent ~$106.41 / WTI ~$103.72. CME FedWatch odds of a Wednesday 25bp hike sat near ~92–93% (CNBC / Reuters). Empire State Manufacturing printed 7.6 in September vs 20.6 in August, with prices paid rising further (WSJ / Dow Jones Newswires). Asia was soft overnight (MSCI Asia ex-Japan −0.12% earlier Reuters wrap); into the US open, Markets Insider had S&P 500 futures ~7,623.50 (−0.02%) and Nasdaq-100 futures ~29,131.75 (−0.07%).

The AI conversation pushed back on mechanism, not on Monday's price action. Hock Tan on CNBC Mad Money said pacing chatter had not changed Broadcom's AI semiconductor forecasts — $115bn FY27 / $230bn FY28 — and called infrastructure demand "very strong" and "very durable" (`2026-09-15-broadcom-tan-ai-revenue-targets`); X re-centered the same path against "cycle end" takes (`2026-09-15-broadcom-ai-revenue-vs-cycle-end`). Tanay Jaipuria's Anthropic Broadcom TPU roadmap — 1 GW / 5 GW / line of sight to 10 more GW — drew a thin but sharp "how will they pay" reply (`2026-09-15-anthropic-broadcom-gw-financeability`). Korean wires on Samsung Taylor ~30% utilization / Tesla AI5 pull-forward and Samsung–TSMC custom HBM base-die dual-sourcing stayed live (`2026-09-15-samsung-taylor-tesla-ai5-ramp`, `2026-09-15-samsung-hbm-base-die-tsmc-dual-source`). SemiAnalysis's Astra loop-transformer claim opened a new HBM-vs-FLOP axis without settling it (`2026-09-15-astra-loop-transformers`). Wells, via Quintanilla, flagged active data-center moratoriums +175% over three months (`2026-09-15-wells-dc-moratoriums-up`). ASR longforms that landed overnight — All-In Jensen ("doomer hoax") and All-In Elon/Shotwell (Terafab / compute rental) — argue sustained build, not cliff (`2026-09-14-allin-jensen-doomer-hoax`, `2026-09-15-allin-elon-shotwell-terafab`). Balchunas's 90% vs 38% hike-odds juxtaposition kept the Fed binary on X (`2026-09-15-fed-hike-odds-juxtaposition`).

Open question for the cash session: does the open trade Monday's AI de-rating as unfinished business, or do oil + a ~5% 10y into Wednesday's Fed (Chair Warsh) dominate the index path while semis digest operator guidance vs essay language?
