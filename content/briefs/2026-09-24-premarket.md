**AI capex durability — financing cleared at scale; no hyperscaler guide-down.** SoftBank’s [~$11.1B](/news/2026-09-21-softbank-openai-junk-bonds) OpenAI HY is now issued (world’s largest HY corporate on record per [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/softbank-issues-111-billion-bonds-openai-financing-push-2026-09-24/)) — allocator access for the Oct 1 tranche, not an offtake print. BlackRock/IFM exclusive talks on [Stack Asia](/news/2026-09-24-blackrock-ifm-stack-asia) up to ~$25B put another large Asia DC ownership process on tape (unsigned). GS’s [Lynam](/longform/2026-09-23-alpha-exchange-lynam-ai-credit) maps hyperscaler IG ~$250bn/’26 → ~$400bn/’27 plus ~$300bn DC/chip project finance in 2027 against still-open IG absorb; SemiAnalysis [ClusterMAX 3.0](/longform/2026-09-23-semianalysis-ep033-clustermax-3) still sees near-term GPU-hour backwardation. Theme falsifier (top-four sequential capex cut / inference cost killing new build) unmet.

**Inference margin inversion — China price *hikes* vs U.S. list cuts; cache math bites realized P&L.** DeepSeek’s [ARR ~$1B](/news/2026-09-24-deepseek-1b-arr) was partly driven by raising model prices **2.3–4.5×** — opposite direction from recent U.S. frontier list cuts already in the archive; China and U.S. lab pricing are not the same market. SemiAnalysis’s endpoint teaser (~75% vs ~99% cache-hit roughly doubling replay cost) [weakens](/longform/2026-09-23-semianalysis-ep033-clustermax-3) the idea that list/serving math alone captures realized inference P&L. No consecutive lab gross-margin decline disclosed.
