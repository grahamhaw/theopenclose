## The read

The concentration split is the useful fact: spend and tokens are not the same market. Walker's OpenRouter aggregate — ~70% of spend vs ~27% of tokens at three major US labs — favors reading frontier APIs as a high-ASP, low-token-share slice rather than "the labs are losing share" on raw volume. What is unresolved is composition (how much Google moves either bar) and whether the gap is price, model mix, or routing. Reply chain is thin; do not overfit.
