## Opening

**SoftBank launched senior unsecured notes of $10 billion plus €1 billion (~$1.15 billion) to fund its OpenAI investment, a term sheet showed Monday, per Reuters.**
[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/softbank-group-launches-over-10-billion-bonds-openai-investment-term-sheet-shows-2026-09-21/) (Kane Wu, Yantoultra Ngui) says dollar notes split across 3-1/2-, 5-1/2-, and 7-1/2-year maturities and euro notes across 4- and 6-year maturities. Proceeds fund SoftBank's $10 billion third-tranche OpenAI follow-on expected to close October 1 and general corporate purposes, and cancel a prior $10 billion bridge. SoftBank declined comment on the term sheet. Pricing is expected September 24 with settlement September 29; Fitch assigned BB+. Bloomberg's "$20B early interest" headline appeared on the desk RSS; the full Bloomberg article was not opened in this capture.
