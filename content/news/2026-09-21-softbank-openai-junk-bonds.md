## Opening

**SoftBank launched senior unsecured notes of $10 billion plus €1 billion (~$1.15 billion) to fund its OpenAI investment, a term sheet showed Monday, per Reuters.**
[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/softbank-group-launches-over-10-billion-bonds-openai-investment-term-sheet-shows-2026-09-21/) (Kane Wu, Yantoultra Ngui) says dollar notes split across 3-1/2-, 5-1/2-, and 7-1/2-year maturities and euro notes across 4- and 6-year maturities. Proceeds fund SoftBank's $10 billion third-tranche OpenAI follow-on expected to close October 1 and general corporate purposes, and cancel a prior $10 billion bridge. SoftBank declined comment on the term sheet. Pricing is expected September 24 with settlement September 29; Fitch assigned BB+. Bloomberg's "$20B early interest" headline appeared on the desk RSS; the full Bloomberg article was not opened in this capture.

## Why it matters

Separate from SoftBank's Arm share-backed margin loan upsizing: this is cash for a dated OpenAI payment and bridge takeout. Funding flexibility for an AI allocator, not an offtake or GPU absorption print.

## Update — 2026-09-24

**Issued, not just launched — CNBC.** SoftBank said it **issued** $10 billion of dollar senior notes and €1 billion (~$1.14 billion) of euro notes (~$11.1 billion combined). Proceeds fund the **$10 billion** payment for the **third and final tranche** of SoftBank’s **$30 billion** OpenAI follow-on (expected close **Oct 1**) and general corporate purposes, per [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/24/softbank-shares-bond-issuance-openai.html) (Jenny Lee). SoftBank shares jumped **more than 7%** Thursday as Japanese markets reopened after a three-day holiday. CNBC adds the February $30 billion commitment would bring SoftBank’s cumulative OpenAI investment to **$64.6 billion** and an approximately **13%** stake upon completion.
