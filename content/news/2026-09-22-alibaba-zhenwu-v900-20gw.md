## Opening

**At Apsara in Hangzhou, Alibaba unveiled the Zhenwu V900 AI chip, set a >20 GW global Cloud data-center capacity target by 2032, and outlined a Qwen path to 5–10 trillion parameters, per Reuters.**
[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/alibaba-plans-ai-model-with-5-trillion-10-trillion-parameters-unveils-new-chip-2026-09-22/) reports CEO Eddie Wu said the T-Head Zhenwu V900 delivers three times the performance of the prior M890 and can cluster up to 500,000 chips, with mass production and commercial release in Q1 2027. Current flagship Qwen 3.8 Max is cited at 2.4 trillion parameters; Qwen 4 is in training, with future Qwen 4.5/5 expected to scale toward 5–10 trillion. Wu said industry mid-to-long-term demand "far outpaces" supply and Cloud will bring AI supernodes online at commercial scale this quarter. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/22/alibaba-ai-alibabacloud-zhenwu-v900-.html) adds existing Zhenwu chips are used by more than 650 customers. Hong Kong-listed shares rose about 5.1% on the day per Reuters (CNBC cited around 3%).

## The numbers
