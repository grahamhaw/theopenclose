## Opening

**SK Hynix's U.S. NAND unit Solidigm is considering its first U.S. NAND flash mass-production fab, with upstate New York a leading site candidate.**
[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/sk-hynixs-solidigm-unit-is-weighing-nand-memory-chip-factory-us-sources-say-2026-09-18/) (Hyunjoo Jin, Heekyong Yang, Karen Freifeld), citing three people familiar, says the project would be **separate** from SK Hynix's talks with Intel to make memory at Intel's Ohio facility. SK Hynix said Solidigm is "reviewing various options to strengthen its business competitiveness, but no specific plans have been confirmed." No agreement on investment details yet, one source cautioned. A U.S. plant would reduce reliance on Solidigm's sole NAND fab in Dalian, China, and help versus tariffs / equipment-export risk (Reuters analysis via sources).
