## Opening

**Nscale filed to list on the NYSE under NSCL — a London AI cloud / GPU rental neocloud with Nvidia, Dell, and Point72 among investors, CNBC reports from the prospectus.**
[CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/nscale-ai-cloud-provider-ipo-nscl.html) (Jordan Novet) says the S-1 shows $140.6 million revenue and a $1.02 billion net loss for the six months ended June 30, 2026, vs $10.4 million revenue and a $368.9 million loss a year earlier (~1,252% revenue growth). Remaining performance obligations are cited at $56.4 billion. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Microsoft are named as customers; one unnamed customer was over half of H1 revenue. As of Aug. 31: >1,000 FTEs, 25,000 active GPUs and 461,000 active-or-contracted GPUs, five active / twelve contracted DC sites, line of sight to 10 GW, and more than $8 billion of debt excluding a Dell financing arrangement. March funding valued the firm at $14.6 billion; Nvidia agreed to guarantee up to $860 million on a Texas data-center lease, per the filing via CNBC. Share count and price range are not set.

## Why it matters

Puts another Nvidia-tied neocloud’s contracted GPU backlog and loss scale on a public S-1 — comparable to the CoreWeave / Crusoe financing tape — with concentration risk explicit (one customer >50% H1 rev) and a hyperscaler-adjacent guarantee on a large lease.

## Positioning

**AI capex durability** — Contracted GPU/RPO and 10 GW LOS are consistent with continued capacity demand; the break would be RPO write-downs or customer cancelations in later filings, not the act of filing.
