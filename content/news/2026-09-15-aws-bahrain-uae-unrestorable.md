## Opening

**AWS told customers Tuesday it cannot restore access to resources and data hosted exclusively in its Bahrain region after drone-related damage exceeded what its services are designed to withstand.** A separate update said resources and data in one UAE availability zone are similarly unrecoverable; AWS continues work on two other affected UAE zones. CNBC notes this is AWS's first public update on the incidents since April; Amazon had said in March that two UAE data centers were directly struck and a Bahrain facility was damaged by a nearby strike. IRGC claimed responsibility. Further Bahrain customer updates expected early 2027; UAE updates "in the coming months."

## The numbers

**UAE Stargate (May 2025 partners OpenAI, Nvidia, Oracle, Cisco) — expected 5 GW / ~10 square miles** — cited by CNBC as context for Gulf AI campus risk; UAE considering dispersing sites and hardening (underground, blast-resistant concrete, interceptors) per Reuters via CNBC.

## Why it matters

Permanent regional data loss is a concrete hyperscaler geo-risk print into the Gulf AI buildout story — distinct from soft "eyes Canada" partnership talk the same afternoon.
