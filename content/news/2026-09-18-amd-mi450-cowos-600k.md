## Opening

**Paradis relays a Morgan Stanley-framed AMD / TSMC / OSAT channel check: MI450/MI455 stay 100% on TSMC CoWoS in 2027 at a mid-point ~600k units for both SKUs.**
[@ParadisLabs](https://x.com/ParadisLabs/status/2100897686702440944) also says ASE/Amkor CoWoS-adjacent capacity ramps are rumoured slower than expected, which could force TSMC to support CoWoS-L wafers for AMD Venice CPU production and further constrain MI450/MI455 — "~80k CoWoS-L for Venice" per MS in the same post. Primary MS note not opened this pass; treat as secondhand relay. Visible replies mostly agree/amplify the CoWoS gate (no contrary supply figure in the skimmed top replies).

## The numbers

**MI450/MI455 — mid-point ~600k units next year; 100% TSMC CoWoS in 2027 — MS via Paradis.**
**Venice — ~80k CoWoS-L wafers if TSMC must cover OSAT shortfall — MS via Paradis.**

## Why it matters

If accurate, AMD's 2027 AI GPU unit path remains packaging-gated at TSMC, and Venice CPU packaging competes for the same scarce CoWoS-L pool — a constraint channel for AMD AI shipments independent of end-demand chatter.

**HBM supply binds** — Adjacent packaging constraint: CoWoS/OSAT tightness can bind accelerator shipments even when HBM is the usual binding narrative.
