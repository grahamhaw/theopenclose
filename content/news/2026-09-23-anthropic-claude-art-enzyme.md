## Opening

**Anthropic says Claude autonomously found a novel bacteriophage enzyme system with CRISPR-like DNA repeats — function unknown; Feng Zhang calls the RNA-repeat / reverse-transcriptase pairing “genuinely intriguing.”**
[Anthropic’s Sep 23 post](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system) introduces a Bay Area life-sciences lab (BSL-1/BSL-2; humans run wet lab) and reports early results: Claude agents, given a high-level prompt to mine reverse transcriptases (RTs), flagged a jumbo-phage RT with an adjacent evenly spaced DNA-repeat array and an accessory protein — a pattern Anthropic names **array-associated reverse transcriptases (ART)**. The underlying RT had appeared in prior studies; Anthropic says Claude first noticed the associated non-coding repeat array. After lab expression work, Anthropic says the array is expressed as distinct short RNAs. [Dario Amodei’s X post](https://x.com/DarioAmodei/status/2102831170299834652) stresses utility and significance are **not yet clear**, and frames biology as on a similar multi-year “weak → superhuman” curve as math. The post quotes Feng Zhang (MIT / Broad) reviewing a pre-print.

## The numbers

**Search campaign — ~950 agents, ~210 million tokens, ~21 hours over a massive RT DNA database — Anthropic primary.**

**Candidate funnel — >200,000 RTs gathered → ~3,500 new candidate systems → 20 most-compelling human-readable reports — Anthropic primary.**

**Wet lab — human scientists only at BSL-1/BSL-2; no autonomous equipment control claimed today — Anthropic primary.**

## Why it matters

This is a capability / go-to-market signal for Anthropic’s life-sciences push ahead of the expected IPO window already in the archive: an end-to-end claim that agents can mine genomes, propose candidates, and hand humans a wet-lab verification loop in weeks. It does **not** establish a programmable gene-editing tool — Anthropic and Zhang both mark function as open. Self-serving framing is the product demo itself; the Zhang quote is third-party but invited.
