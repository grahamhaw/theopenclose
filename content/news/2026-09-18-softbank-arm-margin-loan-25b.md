## Opening

**SoftBank increased its Arm share-backed margin loan by $5 billion to $25 billion, per Bloomberg people familiar.**
[Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-18/softbank-raises-arm-margin-loan-to-25-billion-as-ai-bets-grow) (Kari Soo Lindberg, Min-Jeong Lee) reports SoftBank renegotiated terms and signed with creditors this month to fund expanding AI investments; primary company release not opened in this capture. A [TradingView Reuters headline relay](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2026:newsml_FWN4590UH:0-softbank-has-increased-its-margin-loan-backed-by-shares-of-its-chip-unit-arm-holdings-by-5-billion-to-25-billion-bloomberg-news/) restates the same $5B / $25B figures. Separately, [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-18/softbank-boosts-credit-line-to-6-5-billion-as-ai-debts-mount) (Janice Huang) says SoftBank secured an additional $450 million, lifting an existing credit line from about $6.05 billion to $6.5 billion and extending drawdown another year; whether the line is drawn is unclear.

## The numbers

**Arm margin loan — $25B (+$5B) — Bloomberg people familiar.**
**Credit line — $6.5B (from ~$6.05B; +$450M) — Bloomberg people familiar.**

## Why it matters

Collateralized Arm equity and an upsized short-term credit line are funding tools for SoftBank's AI commitments (including OpenAI-linked facilities referenced in prior Bloomberg coverage), not offtake demand prints. Treat as balance-sheet flexibility, not proof of GPU absorption.

## Positioning

**AI capex durability** — Financing capacity for a major AI allocator; does not by itself settle whether hyperscaler/lab offtake holds.
