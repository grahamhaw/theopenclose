## Opening

**Texas Gov. Greg Abbott directed TCEQ on Sep 21 to halt all permits sought by data-center projects until ERCOT completes its review.**
The [Sep 21 letter](https://gov.texas.gov/uploads/files/press/TCEQ_Data_Center.pdf) to TCEQ Executive Director Kelly Keel says no other state agency should move forward on data-center regulatory approvals until PUC/ERCOT/TWDB audit information is in hand, and asks TCEQ for a compliance update by Monday, October 19, 2026. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/21/texas-governor-abbott-ai-data-center-permits.html) and the [Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/09/21/texas-data-center-moratorium-water-energy/) report this expands Abbott's August moratorium on new ERCOT grid approvals into environmental permitting — closing a path some behind-the-meter / non-ERCOT projects could use to bypass the grid freeze.

## The numbers

**Compliance update due — Oct 19, 2026 — Abbott letter to TCEQ.**
**BloombergNEF (via CNBC, Aug analysis of the earlier Texas pause) — "almost 20%" of a U.S. 253-GW data-center pipeline at risk, or nearly 50 GW; up to $8B data-center revenue losses by Q1 2027 — CNBC attribution, not restated in the Sep 21 letter.**

## Why it matters

State-level Texas friction is no longer only interconnection. Treat BNFF pipeline-at-risk figures as analysis of the August pause, not as a new Sep 21 official capacity cut.
