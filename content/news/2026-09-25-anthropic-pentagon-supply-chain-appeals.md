## Opening

**A 2-1 D.C. Circuit panel on Friday upheld the Defense Department’s March designation of Anthropic as a supply-chain risk, preserving the ban on Claude in DoD systems and in contractor work for the agency — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/25/pentagon-anthropic-ai-risk-appeals-court.html); [WIRED](https://www.wired.com/story/appeals-court-lets-the-pentagon-designate-anthropic-a-supply-chain-risk/).**
Judge Gregory Katsas wrote for the majority (joined by Judge Neomi Rao); Judge Karen LeCraft Henderson dissented. The panel said the Department had “ample support” that continued integration of Claude by DoD or its contractors presented a covered national-security risk. Anthropic had challenged the action as arbitrary, unauthorized, and unconstitutional after talks over GenAI.mil collapsed — DoD seeking broad lawful-purpose access, Anthropic refusing use for fully autonomous weapons and domestic mass surveillance. A Northern District of California court vacated a **parallel** designation under a different statute last month; Friday’s ruling leaves the D.C.-track designation in place. An Anthropic spokesperson told CNBC the company “respectfully disagree[s],” notes the California holding, and is “considering all options, including further review.” The panel delayed immediate effect to allow rehearing / en banc / Supreme Court petitions.

## The numbers

**Vote — 2–1 (Katsas/Rao majority; Henderson dissent) — CNBC / WIRED.**

**Prior DoD contract color — Anthropic signed a $200 million Pentagon contract in July 2025; GenAI.mil deployment talks later collapsed — CNBC.**

**Dual-track litigation — two distinct statutory designations litigated in separate courts; N.D. Cal. vacated one; D.C. Circuit upheld the other — CNBC / WIRED.**

## Why it matters

Keeps a live federal-procurement bar on Claude for DoD and defense-contractor workflows even after a California vacatur of a sibling label — split authority into a years-long appeal path, with IPO-window optics for Anthropic and substitution pressure toward other frontier vendors on the military side.
