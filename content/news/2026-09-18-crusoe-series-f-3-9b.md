## Opening

**Crusoe closed an initial $3.9 billion Series F at a $30.9 billion post-money valuation.**
[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/ai-infrastructure-provider-crusoe-valued-309-billion-latest-funding-round-2026-09-17/) reports the AI infrastructure / neocloud provider said Thursday it raised $3.9 billion; the round was co-led by Atreides Management, Mubadala Capital, and Valor Equity Partners, with Founders Fund, Nvidia, and QIA among backers. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/crusoe-raises-3-9b-to-build-massive-data-centers-and-small-modular-ai-factories/) (Marina Temkin) matches the figures and adds GIC, Radical Ventures, and TPG; proceeds fund existing projects (including Abilene, Texas used by OpenAI) and smaller modular "Spark" AI factories that can be trucked and connected to power without large construction crews. Prior raise: $1.38 billion at $10 billion valuation about 10 months earlier (TechCrunch).
